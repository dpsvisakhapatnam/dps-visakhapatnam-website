MOTTO

Service before self

Philosophy: Develop all four human dimensions Body, Mind, Heart and Soul

To teach to learn for: The Body to live, Mind to learn, Heart to love, Soul to Leave a Legacy

Objective: To enable students to excel in academics and also recognize their full potential and develop good habits so that they can contribute towards the progress of the world. To encourage to utilize the three great gifts given by the God to all human beings:
Power of Choice, Power of Intelligence, Power of Conscience

Aim

Our aim is to impart sound and liberal education to boys and girls during their impressionable years type of education that would lay stress on character building, team work, esprit-de-corps, physical development and to infuse in them a spirit of adventure,fair play and justice.
We aim to: Develop among our students a feeling of pride in Indian culture and produce citizens will be truly Indian and will be above social,communal, religious or provincial prejudices.
