About the school

Brief introduction

Delhi Public School Visakhapatnam was established in the year 1991 and is now imparting quality education with the most modern teaching techniques available to enable its students to compete with the world. It provides a congenial atmosphere for the total development of the whole personality and its integration with the society. It is committed to academic excellence, and appreciation of the worth of the individual student and educational leadership.

The school tries to inculcate in students the values of personal hygiene, neatness in uniform, cleanliness of surroundings, graceful manners, obedience and discipline. The children are provided with an environment which fosters a spirit of enquiry and keen competition. Personal attention is given to each individual child. A competent and supporting staff takes care of the special needs of the children with full commitment.

Learning is a joy here and the school provides a very vibrant and inspiring atmosphere for the students to grow in a stress-free environment.

What we believe

Motto, philosophy & aim

Philosophy

Motto: Service before Self.

To develop all four human dimensions — Body, Mind, Heart and Soul. To teach to learn for: the Body to live, Mind to learn, Heart to love, Soul to leave a legacy.

To enable students to excel in academics and recognise their full potential and develop good habits so that they can contribute towards the progress of the world — utilising the three great gifts given by God to all human beings: Power of Choice, Power of Intelligence, Power of Conscience.

Aim

Our aim is to impart sound and liberal education to boys and girls during their impressionable years — education that would lay stress on character building, team work, esprit-de-corps, physical development and infuse in them a spirit of adventure, fair play and justice.

We aim to develop among our students a feeling of pride in Indian culture and produce citizens who will be truly Indian and above social, communal, religious or provincial prejudices.

International Policy

To encourage young minds to develop understanding, tolerance and appreciation of diverse cultures of the world.

To nurture global citizens who play a responsible role for sustainable development in a changing world.

Principal’s message

Principal speaks

“The will to win, the desire to succeed, the urge to reach your full potential… these are the keys that will unlock the door to personal excellence.”

Confucius couldn’t have summed up better what we at DPS wish for every student that steps into our portals. DPS Visakhapatnam dates back to 1991 when the school was set up in the Steel Township under the aegis of the Delhi Public School Society, New Delhi. From then onwards, the school has grown in strength with the continuous support of Visakhapatnam Steel Plant Management, who readily lend a helping hand for our upkeep and smooth functioning. The DPS Society too has rendered invaluable support with its guidance, teacher training programmes, Inter DPS Competitions etc.

The school curriculum provides ample opportunities to go beyond tests and textbooks. Students can foray into numerous activities — be it learning a new language, a new dance form, a new prayer song, a new technique of painting or it could be just witnessing a well orchestrated Morning Assembly — each a new experience, a new joy, all helping to nurture and hone new skills. At DPS, we are ever committed to enable every child to grow to his fullest, which undoubtedly leads to an exhilarating sense of achievement. Added to this, is the spirit of camaraderie and bonhomie fostered through Inter-House competitions and sports activities.

A well stocked library that is replenished periodically is the DPS way of motivating children to rediscover the pleasure of reading. Books are indeed a “unique portable magic” that transport you from one world to another and thereby open a whole new realm of “Light and Wisdom”.

“Children are our priority, change is the reality, collaboration is our strategy.” Technology and automation have been incorporated in the school system, ensuring faster and more convenient way of interaction with our stakeholders. Digital classrooms have been introduced to equip teachers and students with 21st century skills, necessary for all to be lifelong learners. Thus, DPS re-affirms that we need to be adaptive and accommodating in this changing world. It is only a collaborative effort of teachers and parents that can lay out the right path, where students are prepared to face all challenges of the world.

S K Pandey
Principal, DPS Visakhapatnam

Serving the field of education

Delhi Public School has been serving the Society in the field of education for the last 68 years. There are presently over 200 Schools under its aegis both in and outside India. The growing number of schools under this society signifies how the need to provide meaningful education to a large number of children desirous of growing into educated and liberated human beings has been fulfilled. Founded and managed by distinguished members and luminaries of society culled from various walks of life, the organisation has always guided its member schools on the path of progress with their visionary approach and lofty ideals. Consequently, Delhi Public Schools have become significant portals of learning and growth of young minds.

The main aim of this society is to provide an all-round development and sound moral base. Fostering character building and nurturing the inquisitive spirit of children, encouraging scientific temperament and strengthening social bonds, are constant deeds here. Mr. V. K. Shunglu, Chairman, DPS Society is the constant beacon and source of strength.

From a small beginning in the President’s Estate in 1941 as Church High School to Naveen Bharat School in 1947 and finally in 1949 at its present location of Mathura Road, DPS has traversed a long journey. The birth of a new nation in 1947 was in a way the beginning of DPS (called Naveen Bharat then). A vital time in the history of our nation, when a beginning was made to fulfil a social, national need. DPS was not a silent witness to the trials and tribulations of the growing metropolis but contributed richly to the growth of the capital especially academics. DPS Mathura Road was the only DPS till 1972. But the parent DPS was ready to spread its wings. The many offsprings, 95 schools as on 1st September 2002 that followed from R. K. Puram in 1972 are simple assertions of the strength that is DPS. Yet what has remained unchanged is the zest and spirit to achieve and excel. The society is managing and maintaining a uniform level of excellence, hallmark of a Delhi Public School, with schools set up over vast regions with diverse cultural backgrounds, socio-economic conditions and educational contrasts. This diversity is respected while ensuring that all the schools under the aegis of the society follow a uniform educational practice.

World famous DPS teachers’ work ethic and dynamism is the bedrock on which rests the DPS edifice. Highly qualified, talented faculty with right skills and positive attitude goes beyond the call of duty to provide appropriately contextual learning experiences to young minds. The DPS faculty boasts of University top rankers and winners of national and international awards. These teachers have committed their working lives to an endeavour centred on building new generations. In-service teacher education programmes, national and international assignments and exposure visits ensure professional and personal growth of these lifelong learners.
