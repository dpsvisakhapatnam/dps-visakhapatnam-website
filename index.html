About the school

A place where learning is a joy

Delhi Public School Visakhapatnam was established in 1991 and imparts quality education with the most modern teaching techniques, so its students can compete with the world. It provides a congenial atmosphere for the total development of the whole personality and its integration with society.

The school inculcates personal hygiene, neatness, cleanliness, graceful manners and discipline, in an environment that fosters a spirit of enquiry and keen competition — with personal attention to every child and a competent, supportive staff.

Motto: Service before Self

To develop all four human dimensions — Body, Mind, Heart and Soul.

Our objective is to enable students to excel in academics, recognise their full potential and develop good habits so that they can contribute to the progress of the world — using the three great gifts given to every human being: the Power of Choice, the Power of Intelligence and the Power of Conscience.

Our aim is to impart sound and liberal education to boys and girls during their impressionable years — an education that lays stress on character building, team work, esprit-de-corps and physical development, and infuses a spirit of adventure, fair play and justice.

We aim to develop among our students a feeling of pride in Indian culture, and to produce citizens who are truly Indian and above social, communal, religious or provincial prejudices.

To encourage young minds to develop understanding, tolerance and appreciation of the diverse cultures of the world.

To nurture global citizens who play a responsible role in sustainable development in a changing world.

Why DPS Visakhapatnam

Everything a child needs to grow

From classrooms and labs to playgrounds and stages — a campus built for the whole child.

Consistent academic excellence

100% pass in Classes X and XII every year from 2020-21 to 2025-26, with toppers scoring 98.8% and 96.8%.

Well-equipped laboratories

Separate Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths and Computer Science labs for hands-on learning.

Digital classrooms

Technology and automation built into the school system, equipping students with 21st-century skills.

A library that is always growing

Over 6,000 reference books, 12 magazines, 4 periodicals and 5 dailies — replenished regularly.

Sprawling playground

More than 32,000 sq. m of playground, indoor games, and a full calendar of sports and Inter-House events.

Arts, music & dance

Dedicated music and dance rooms, art and painting, dramatics, creative writing and public speaking.

International dimension

Recognised by the British Council for an International Dimension in Schools (2026–2029).

Character first

Service before Self: assemblies, house system, scholar awards and a clear, fair discipline policy.

Admissions

Join the DPS family

Simple, transparent and personal. Here is how a year of admissions flows.

Dec – Jan

Registration

Names are registered from the second half of December to January.

Feb – Mar

Test & interview

Written test in English, Hindi and Maths. Nursery: a meeting with teachers and the Principal.

After tests

Selection list

The final list of selected candidates is displayed on the School Notice Board.

April

Session begins

An orientation programme is held for new parents; school starts on the first working day of April.
